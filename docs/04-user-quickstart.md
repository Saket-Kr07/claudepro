# 04 - User Quickstart

Welcome to **DoubtDrop**! Follow these 4 simple steps to participate in your lecture anonymously.

---

### Step 1: Understand What DoubtDrop Does
DoubtDrop is a lightweight, zero-login lecture doubt box. It allows you to ask questions about the current lecture without revealing your name, email, or identity to anyone—including your peers and instructor. You can also upvote questions posted by other students so the teacher knows which concepts need extra explanation.

---

### Step 2: What Input to Give
In the **Drop a doubt (anonymous)** panel on the left:
1. **Choose a Topic:** Select the lecture topic that relates to your question from the dropdown menu (e.g., *Binary Trees*, *BFS / DFS*, *Complexity*).
2. **Type Your Question:** Enter your doubt in the question box.
   - Minimum length: **10 characters**
   - Maximum length: **280 characters** (a counter keeps track for you)
3. Click **Post doubt anonymously**.

---

### Step 3: What Result to Expect
- A confirmation toast `✅ Doubt posted anonymously` appears at the bottom of the screen.
- The question form clears automatically.
- Your doubt instantly appears at the top of the **Class doubts** list highlighted with a brief visual glow.
- Other students and the instructor can immediately see your question, and anyone in the class can upvote it by clicking the **▲** button.

---

### Step 4: How to Recover From a Mistake
- **Accidentally upvoted a doubt?**
  Click the **▲** button on that doubt a second time. Your vote will be removed immediately (`↩️ Vote removed`).
- **Clicked the button too quickly?**
  If you see `⏳ Too fast. One vote per question per session.`, simply wait half a second before clicking again.
- **Accidentally typed an incomplete question?**
  If you submit before finishing, an on-screen validation error will guide you (e.g., reminding you to select a topic or reach the 10-character minimum) without erasing your draft text.
- **Duplicate doubt?**
  If someone already asked the exact same question, DoubtDrop notifies you: *This exact doubt is already posted — upvote it instead!* You can simply find the existing doubt in the list and click **▲**.
